# 📚 StudyForge

### From Lecture Overload to Exam-Ready Clarity.

StudyForge is an AI-powered study assistant that transforms lecture PDFs and study material into structured, exam-focused learning resources.

Instead of spending hours reading through lengthy PDFs, students can upload their material and use AI-powered tools to generate concise notes, quick revisions, quizzes, and flashcards.

---

## 🚀 Features

### 📄 AI-Powered PDF Processing
Upload your lecture notes or study PDFs and let StudyForge analyze the content.

### 📝 AI-Generated Notes
Convert lengthy study material into structured and easy-to-understand notes focused on important concepts.

### ⚡ Quick Revision
Get condensed revision material designed for quick preparation before exams.

### 🧠 Practice Quiz
Generate practice questions from your study material to test your understanding.

### 🃏 Flashcards
Turn important concepts into flashcards for active recall and faster revision.

### 🤖 AI Study Copilot
Interact with your study material using an AI-powered study assistant.

### 📊 Study Dashboard
Track important study metrics such as:

- Notes generated
- Revision progress
- Quiz accuracy
- Study streak

### 🎯 Exam-Focused Learning
Specify your subject and exam goal to make the generated learning material more relevant to your preparation.

### 🌙 Modern Dark UI
StudyForge uses a modern, minimal dark interface with smooth animations and a dashboard-style experience.
