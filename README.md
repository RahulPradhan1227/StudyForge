# ✦ StudyForge

### From lecture overload to exam-ready clarity.

StudyForge is an AI-powered student workspace that transforms lengthy lecture PDFs into concise, structured, and exam-focused study material.

Upload a lecture, provide your subject and exam goal, and let StudyForge turn it into revision-ready content.

---

## 🚀 Features

- 📄 **Lecture PDF Upload** — Upload lecture material using drag-and-drop.
- 🧠 **AI Revision Notes** — Convert lectures into structured, exam-focused notes.
- ⚡ **Quick Revision** — Get a concise last-minute revision sheet.
- 📝 **Practice Quiz** — Test your understanding with AI-generated MCQs.
- 📊 **Study Insights** — Identify important concepts, formulas, and exam topics.
- 📥 **Export** — Copy, download, or print your revision material.
- 🎨 **Modern UI** — Clean, responsive glassmorphism-based interface.
